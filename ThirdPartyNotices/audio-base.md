# Base audio sources

Kenney Impact Sounds is CC0. The original distribution notice is retained in `Kenney-Impact-Sounds.txt`.

Additional recordings are from Freesound under CC0-1.0. Source pages and offline processing are recorded in `provenance/audio-base.json`; later source versions are listed in `audio-and-fonts.md` and the remaining provenance files.

Procedural masks and generated bubble sounds are project-authored assets. Files in the provenance archive include superseded derivatives as well as currently used source material.
